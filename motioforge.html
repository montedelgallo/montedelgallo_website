About

A timeline editor for real-world motion.

MotioForge Studio turns a set of servos, control boards and speakers into a programmable animatronic system. You draw movements on a graphical timeline, just like a video or music editor, but instead of frames and notes you work with angular positions and real hardware commands.

Robotic and Environmental Automation for Showmanship.

MotioForge Studio grew out of the MdG Robotic Lab, where the motion authoring software was first built.

What you can do

Motion, light and sound in a single project.

Precise motion sequences

Control over position, speed and acceleration for one or more servos.

Synchronized digital outputs

Trigger switches, relays, LEDs and pneumatic actuators in sync with motion.

Coordinated audio

Play audio files and auto-generate movements synced to voice or music.

Multiple devices in parallel

Manage many devices on a single hardware domain, each on its own port.

Loop or trigger

Play in a continuous loop or fire the animation on manual or software command.

Portable projects

Domains and animations saved as standard files, portable to any machine.

How it works

From hardware wiring to motion in four steps.

01

Define the hardware

You create a domain describing the physical setup: communication ports, connected elements (servos, switches, audio modules) and their parameters: minimum and maximum travel, rest position, hardware model.

02

Create an animation

Inside the domain you add an animation: a project with its own timeline, to run once, in a continuous loop or on an external trigger.

03

Draw the movements

Each servo is a horizontal track. You draw a segment by dragging the mouse: height is the angular position, width is time. Add, edit, copy and refine until you get the behavior you want.

04

Play and fine-tune

One click starts playback: MotioForge Studio sends commands in real time and the character moves exactly as drawn. Stop, reposition the playhead and repeat until it’s right.

Hardware

Five element types, one timeline.

Servo Bus

Serial servos (ST3215 and compatible) on a shared port. Precise control of position, speed and acceleration.

Servo PWM

Standard servos on digital pins or I²C expanders (e.g. PCA9685), driven at 50 Hz.

Switch

Digital output (relay, solenoid, LED, valve). On/off or switched in sync with animation segments.

Audio

File playback, volume control and automatic lip-sync generation (amplitude or phonemes).

Trigger Button

Physical button on DEIMOS hardware: when pressed it fires the configured animation.

Interface

Built for people who work on stage, not in code.

Use cases

Where MotioForge Studio fits.

Theatre & live shows

Animatronic masks, mechanical mouths synced to voice-over, moving tails and ears, motorized eyes.

Parks & attractions

Large-format figures with multiple movements, triggered as visitors pass, with synced environmental effects.

Escape rooms

Objects that react to players, doors that open, paintings that talk: logic and timing handled here.

Museums & exhibits

Prehistoric animals and historical figures with realistic motion, hands-on exhibits with physical feedback.

Retail & window displays

Animated mannequins, rotating displays, seasonal pieces on programmed cycles, no on-site technician needed.

Cosplay & wearables

Motorized wings, automatic visors, robotic tails and accessories with synced servos and LEDs for every performance.

Animated toys

Plush animals that open their eyes, turn their head or move their paws; dolls that talk while moving their mouth; stuffed animals that breathe. Any toy you want to bring to life.

Prototyping & research

Experimental robotic platforms, lab mechanical arms and automated test rigs.

Benefits

Why MotioForge Studio.

No code required

Animations are built graphically: no programming needed.

Real-time preview

Commands reach the hardware during playback, with no compile step.

Multi-channel

Dozens of servos coordinated on a single timeline.

Cross-hardware

Serial servo buses (ST3215 and compatible) and PWM servos on pins or I²C expanders.

Built-in audio

File playback and lip movements generated automatically from the voice.

On-board AI assistant

Contextual help with setup, animations and troubleshooting, online or offline.
