--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} The page of one case study (a document of the `case_studies` collection, see _case_studies/). From top to bottom: the way back to the case study index, sector, title and summary, the cover when there is one, the results as figure tiles, the challenge and the solution side by side, then two columns like a project page: the migrated text, and the facts (client, duration, services, stack) in a narrow column that sits beside it from 900px up. Front matter used here: title, client, sector, summary, challenge, solution, results (a list of {value, label}), stack; and, optional, `cover` + `cover_alt` (no case study of the old site has a picture, so most have none: the figures lead the page instead) and `facts` (a map of label: text, in the language of the document). script/check-build.sh checks all of it. The page shares the project page's classes for what is the same (the way back, the cover, the two-column layout, the facts, the stack chips, the text), see _project-detail.scss; _case-study.scss only styles what is new here (the result tiles, the challenge / solution blocks, quotes in the text). The labels (Results, The challenge, Client, ...) come from _data/i18n/.yml in the language of the document. The way back is a root-relative path, which jekyll-polyglot turns into /it/casi-di-studio/ in the Italian build: it is the Italian path of the index (case_studies.index_path). The variables are prefixed (cs_): the layout is not an include, but the habit keeps t, lang and p out of the way of anything rendered with it. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign cs_lang = page.lang | default: site.active_lang -%} {%- assign cs_t = site.data.i18n[cs_lang].case_studies -%}
