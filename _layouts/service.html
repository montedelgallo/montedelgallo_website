--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} The page of one service (a document of the `services` collection, see _services/). From top to bottom: the way back to the services overview, icon, eyebrow, title and summary, the text, the FAQ (a
per question, when the document has `faq`), the projects that show the service (cards, when the document has `related_projects`), the call to book, the way back again. Front matter used here: title, icon (a symbol of _includes/icons.html, the home's icon for the service), summary, and, optional, `related_projects` (a list of project page_ids; the cards are the projects of the language of the page) and `faq` (a list of {q, a}). `order` and `page_id` are read by the overview and by jekyll-polyglot. script/check-build.sh checks all of it. The labels (All services, FAQ heading, Related work, the call to book) come from _data/i18n/.yml in the language of the document. The way back is a root-relative path, which jekyll-polyglot turns into /it/servizi/ in the Italian build: it is the Italian path of the overview (services.index_path). The page shares the project page's classes for what is the same (the way back, the text, see _project-detail.scss) and the home's call to action band (.cta, _home.scss); _service.scss only styles what is new here. The variables are prefixed (sv_): the project cards are an include and share the page's variables. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign sv_lang = page.lang | default: site.active_lang -%} {%- assign sv_t = site.data.i18n[sv_lang].services -%}
