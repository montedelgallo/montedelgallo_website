--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} The page of one article (a post, see _posts/). From top to bottom: the way back to the blog index, the category labels, title, date and author, the cover when there is one, the text, and the way back again. Front matter used here: title, date, author, categories (slugs, labelled by _data/i18n/.yml blog.categories), and, optional, `cover` + `cover_alt` + `cover_width` + `cover_height` (the ConsultaLa article has no cover picture). `excerpt` is the card text and the meta description (see head.html). script/check-build.sh checks all of it. The page shares the project page's classes for what is the same (the way back, the cover, the text and its styling, see _project-detail.scss); _blog.scss only adds what is new here (the reading column, the date line, pictures and code in the text). The labels come from _data/i18n/.yml in the language of the post. The way back is a root-relative path, which jekyll-polyglot turns into /it/articoli/ in the Italian build: it is the Italian path of the index (blog.index_path). The variables are prefixed (bp_): the layout is not an include, but the habit keeps t, lang and p out of the way of anything rendered with it. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign bp_lang = page.lang | default: site.active_lang -%} {%- assign bp_t = site.data.i18n[bp_lang].blog -%}
