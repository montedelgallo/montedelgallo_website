--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} The page of one project (a document of the `projects` collection, see _projects/). From top to bottom: the way back to the project index, sector, title and summary, the cover, then two columns: the facts (headline metric, year, stack, links out) and the migrated text of the project, and last the gallery when the document has one. The card on the home page and on the project index (_includes/project-card.html) reads the same front matter. Front matter used here: title, sector, summary, cover, cover_alt, metric, year, stack; and, optional, `metrics` (the project's other figures, a list of {value, label}, shown as tiles under the cover with the case study's tile styles), `facts` (a map of client, duration, services, platforms: only the ones the project has, shown beside the year), `links` (a list of {label, url}: the project's website, its repository, a demo video) and `gallery` (a list of {src, alt, caption, width, height}, pictures that sit under the text). script/check-build.sh checks all of it. The labels (Year, Built with, All projects, Gallery) come from _data/i18n/.yml in the language of the document, which is the language being built, except on a fallback copy of another language's document (see lang-url.html). The way back is a root-relative path, which jekyll-polyglot turns into /it/progetti/ in the Italian build: it is the Italian path of the index (projects.index_path), not the English one with a prefix. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign lang = page.lang | default: site.active_lang -%} {%- assign t = site.data.i18n[lang].projects -%} {%- assign pr_fact_keys = "client,duration,services,platforms" | split: "," -%}
