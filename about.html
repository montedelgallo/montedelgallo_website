Our story · Started in Rome in 2019

Perspective first. Then useful software.

MonteDelGallo was born in Rome to help companies turn operational complexity into clear, owned and measurable digital systems.

I. Origin

A studio built against digital noise.

MonteDelGallo started from a simple frustration: many digital projects become heavier than the problems they were meant to solve. More meetings, more features and more layers rarely create better software.

We chose a different shape: a compact senior team that works close to the business, ships useful versions quickly and keeps every decision tied to measurable impact.

The goal is not to write more code. The goal is to build the right system, with the right amount of code.

II. The name

Monte del Gallo is a place of perspective.

Monte del Gallo is a hill in Rome, close to the Gianicolo. From there the city opens up: layers, streets, domes, routes and hidden connections become easier to read.

That is how we approach software. Before choosing a framework, drawing an interface or automating a workflow, we climb high enough to understand the whole landscape.

Rome roots. Product discipline.

A local base with an international way of building: direct, lean and accountable.

III. Who we are

Senior engineering with a lean, creative process.

MonteDelGallo combines creativity and technical depth to deliver solutions that answer real business needs. We focus on the essential: clear architecture, measurable outcomes, fast iterations and software that teams can actually use every day.

Our work sits at the intersection of visibility and operations: websites built to convert, SEO and GEO for search and AI engines, custom management systems, AI agents, e-commerce, cloud and data analysis.

Three choices that keep the work sharp.

Use constraints

I.

Brilliant software is so simple — that’s why it’s so complicated.

The best products in the world feel effortless to use. Behind that effortlessness are thousands of decisions, each one deliberate, each one in service of clarity. We don’t add features — we remove friction. We don’t build complexity — we distill it until only the essential remains.

Less, but better.

II.

We are craftspeople, not factories.

MonteDelGallo was founded in 2019 in Rome with a deliberate choice: stay small. We don’t scale by hiring hundreds. We scale by being excellent. Every person on our team is both a thinker and a maker. There are no spectators here.

We chose the name after Monte del Gallo — the highest point of the Gianicolo, where Rome stretches out beneath you in every direction. Great software requires that kind of perspective. You need to see the whole landscape before writing the first line.

Coded with care in Roma.

III.

Constraints are gifts.

Unlimited budgets produce bloated software. Unlimited timelines produce software that never ships. We embrace constraints — of time, of budget, of scope — because they force creativity. The narrower the frame, the sharper the picture.

When a client tells us “we only have this much time and this much money,” we don’t see a problem. We see the creative brief.

IV.

Technology serves people, not the other way around.

We don’t choose technologies because they’re trendy. We choose them because they’re right. A Rails monolith can outperform a microservices architecture when it’s built with care. A static page can outperform a SPA when simplicity is what the user needs.

The most elegant technology choice is the one your users never think about.

V.

We build with you, not for you.

We’re not vendors. We’re partners. We won’t disappear into a black box and emerge months later with a deliverable. You’ll see every decision, question every trade-off, and feel the product take shape week by week. Your knowledge of the problem and our knowledge of the craft — that’s the combination that produces great work.

VI.

Ship, learn, iterate. Repeat forever.

Software is never finished. The best products are living systems that evolve with their users. We don’t believe in big-bang launches followed by neglect. We believe in continuous delivery, continuous learning, and continuous improvement. Ship early. Listen hard. Make it better.

VII.

Code is a team sport.

Lone geniuses are a myth. The best software comes from diverse minds challenging each other — designers who code, engineers who sketch, strategists who prototype. We hire people who are curious about everything, not just their specialty.

We share what we learn. We open-source what we can. We write about our mistakes as openly as our successes. Knowledge compounds when it’s shared.

This is what we believe

Build less. Build better.

If this resonates with you — whether as a client, a collaborator, or a future teammate — we should talk.

How we work · Lean delivery · Measurable work

How we turn ideas into reality.

Every great product follows a path. Ours has been refined across 50+ projects: four phases designed to minimize risk and maximize value.

Understand → Design → Build → Launch. A clear process keeps the work calm: each phase has a purpose, a concrete output and a decision point before we move forward.

01

Discovery

Before we write a single line of code, we need to understand your world. We sit with you, ask hard questions, map the landscape, and uncover what success actually looks like.

  • Stakeholder interviews & workshops
  • Technical landscape audit
  • User research & persona mapping
  • Scope definition & success metrics

Duration: 1–2 weeks

02

Strategy & Design

We architect the system, design the experience, and plan every sprint. You’ll see wireframes, prototypes, and technical specs — and approve each before we move forward.

  • System architecture & tech selection
  • Wireframes & interactive prototypes
  • UI design (pixel-perfect mockups)
  • Sprint plan & milestone roadmap

Duration: 2–3 weeks

03

Development

This is where the magic happens. Agile sprints, continuous integration, and regular demos keep you in control. You’ll feel the product taking shape, week by week.

  • 2-week agile sprints with demos
  • Continuous integration & testing
  • Code reviews & quality assurance
  • Staging environment for your review

Duration: 4–16 weeks (depends on scope)

04

Launch & Beyond

Launch day is a beginning, not an end. We deploy to production, monitor performance, gather real user feedback, and iterate. We stay with you as long as you need us.

  • Production deployment & monitoring
  • Performance optimization
  • User feedback analysis & iteration
  • Ongoing maintenance & support packages

Duration: Ongoing
