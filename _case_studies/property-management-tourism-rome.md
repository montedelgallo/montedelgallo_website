## Overview

A growing property management company in Rome needed a complete platform overhaul to scale bookings, reduce operational errors, and improve communication with guests and property owners.

## What we built

We mapped the full workflow from inquiry to checkout, then built one custom platform around it:

- Real-time availability sync
- Automated guest communication
- An owner dashboard
- Payment flows
- Maintenance tracking
- A mobile workflow for on-ground staff

## Results in six months

Within six months, the platform produced a 140% increase in total bookings, 65% growth in direct bookings, a 90% reduction in double bookings, and a 50% improvement in operational efficiency.
