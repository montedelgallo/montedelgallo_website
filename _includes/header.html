{%- comment -%} The top of every page: the utility strip (location, phone, language switch) and the sticky navigation (brand, links, booking button). The links come from _data/nav.yml, their labels from _data/i18n/.yml. On a phone (below 860px) the links, the language switch and the booking button live in #site-menu, which the button .nav__toggle opens and closes (assets/js/nav.js, styles in _sass/foundry/_shell.scss). The menu is collapsed only when the page has ; without JavaScript (or when nav.js fails to load or throws, which both put the class back to no-js) it is simply open. The language switch is in the utility strip on a desktop and in the menu on a phone. The booking button exists twice so that it stays one tap away on a phone: .nav__book in the bar (phone only, short label) and the one in .nav__cta (desktop only); only one is displayed at a time, so there is never a double tab stop. The link of the section the page belongs to carries aria-current="page" (see `also:` in _data/nav.yml: a project page marks Work, an article marks Blog, a case study marks Work), and on the home the brand link does (the home has no link of its own in the navigation). The 404 and the legal pages mark none. The links are plain root-relative paths on purpose: in the Italian build jekyll-polyglot puts /it/ in front of them. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign lang = site.active_lang -%} {%- assign t = site.data.i18n[lang] -%} {%- assign company = site.data.site_meta.company -%}
