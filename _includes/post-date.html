{%- comment -%} The date of an article, written the way the language writes it: "January 9, 2025" in English, "9 gennaio 2025" in Italian. The month names and the order of the parts come from _data/i18n/.yml (blog.months, blog.date_format), so one source renders both languages. It prints a element, for machines and for the person reading. {% raw %}{% include post-date.html date=post.date lang=post.lang %}{% endraw %} `date` is the date of the post and `lang` the language to write it in (the post's own `lang`). The variables are prefixed (pd_): Liquid's `assign` is global, and an include shares the variables of the page that includes it, so an `assign t`, `lang` or `p` here would replace the page's own (see _includes/project-card.html and the canary in script/check-build.sh). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign pd_blog = site.data.i18n[include.lang].blog -%} {%- assign pd_month = include.date | date: "%-m" | minus: 1 -%} {%- assign pd_day = include.date | date: "%-d" -%} {%- assign pd_year = include.date | date: "%Y" -%} {%- assign pd_text = pd_blog.date_format | replace: "{day}", pd_day | replace: "{month}", pd_blog.months[pd_month] | replace: "{year}", pd_year -%} {{ pd_text | escape }}
