It has no `lang:` in its front matter (that is what makes it single-source, see _includes/lang-url.html), and every word on it comes from the home: tree of _data/i18n/.yml. The sections are includes, in page order, each a port of the same section of the approved mockup (design/home-foundry-mockup.html). RULE FOR EVERYTHING IN _includes/home/: an include runs in THIS page's context and shares its variables, and Liquid's `assign` is global. `t` is the whole i18n tree of the language being built, and every section reads from it, so an include that assigns `t`, `lang` or `p` blanks every section after it, with no error. Read `t`, never assign it.
