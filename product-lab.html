DATA GATHER

Ruby on Rails, PostgreSQL

Data gathering platform for the medical research project “ARDIC”. Enables researchers to collect, organize, and analyze clinical data with full compliance and audit trails.

SaaS / WordPress

Saladil

PHP, WordPress, React

A customer support ticket system built as a WordPress plugin. Lightweight, privacy-first, and designed for small businesses who need a simple help desk without the bloat.
