## Stores that sell and keep running

We build seamless, scalable e-commerce solutions that improve the shopping experience and drive sales. That covers the storefront customers see and the operations behind it: checkout, payments, inventory, and the automation that connects them.

- **Online stores** that are fast and built mobile-first.
- **Checkout optimization**, to remove the friction that makes customers leave.
- **Operational automation** between the store, inventory, CRM and logistics systems.
- **Digital products and subscriptions**: payments, recurring plans, dashboards and user management for scalable digital products.

## What it looked like for a fashion brand

An Italian fashion brand had an online store that crashed during peak sales, loaded slowly on mobile and was disconnected from inventory. We rebuilt the platform with a responsive storefront, real-time inventory synchronization, search and recommendations, and a cloud pipeline for safer releases. Online sales grew by 150%, cart abandonment fell by 40%, pages loaded in about two seconds instead of eight and mobile conversions rose by 50%. Read the full [case study](/case-studies/ecommerce-platform/).

## Subscriptions and payments

For products that charge on a plan, [PhotoAlbum](/projects/photoalbum-retro-photo-sharing-saas/) shows the other side of commerce: subscription tiers handled with Stripe, alongside video processing and album sharing.

## How we work

We start from how the business sells today, build the parts that need to be custom, and measure the outcome in orders, conversion and operating effort.
