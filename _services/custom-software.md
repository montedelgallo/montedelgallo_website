## What we build

Web, desktop and mobile software tailored to your exact specifications and built from scratch around the way your business actually works. High-performance, scalable systems rather than a template bent to fit.

- **Internal tools and operational platforms** that replace spreadsheets, disconnected apps and manual hand-offs.
- **SaaS products** with accounts, roles, payments and dashboards, from the first version to the tenth.
- **Dashboards and reporting** that put the numbers your team needs in front of them.

Some of our own work shows the range: [Soshiki](/projects/soshiki-multi-tenant-crm-platform/), a multi-tenant CRM with LLM-powered email processing; [BistroFlow](/projects/bistroflow-real-time-restaurant-pos/), a restaurant system that updates in real time across every device; and [Gestione Immobili](/projects/gestione-immobili-property-portfolio-manager/), a property portfolio manager with financial dashboards and mapping. For clients, see how we built a [management system for a 15-location restaurant chain](/case-studies/restaurant-management-software-case-study/) and a [booking platform for tourism operations in Rome](/case-studies/property-management-tourism-rome/).

## From the operational problem to a working product

We start from concrete goals, design clear flows and build in measurable iterations. Design, development and growth stay in one team, so strategy and implementation are not separated.

1. **Map** your data, tools and the workflows your team repeats every day.
2. **Build** the custom software and integrations the work actually needs.
3. **Automate** the manual steps that remain, with AI where it pays off.
4. **Measure** real outcomes and keep improving what moves the numbers.

## Modernizing and maintaining

Software rarely starts from a blank page. We handle cloud migration, the redesign of legacy systems and process automation to modernize a business without stopping it. After launch, ongoing maintenance, support and optimization keep the software performing at its best.

## The stack

We are polyglot engineers and choose the tool for the job. For the products above that usually means Ruby on Rails, Hotwire, Tailwind CSS and PostgreSQL. Beyond that, our toolkit covers:

- **Frontend:** React, Vue.js, Svelte, Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS.
- **Backend and APIs:** Node.js, Python, Ruby on Rails, Go, PHP and Laravel, Django.
- **Mobile:** Swift, Kotlin, React Native and Flutter.
- **Cloud and DevOps:** AWS, Google Cloud, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD and Terraform.
- **Data and AI:** TensorFlow, PyTorch, OpenAI and other LLMs, PostgreSQL, Apache Kafka and BigQuery.

Technology is a means, not an end: the best choice is the one your users never think about.
