## From idea to App Store

We turn your idea into a polished, production-ready mobile application, whether that is a native iOS experience, an Android-first solution or a cross-platform app that runs everywhere. We cover the complete lifecycle, with a strong focus on UX: great apps are not just functional, they are a joy to use.

Our apps are not built in isolation. They connect to your backend, your data and your operational systems, with attention to performance, security and integration with what you already run.

## Every platform, one team

- **iOS native:** Swift and SwiftUI for high-performance apps that use the full power of Apple's ecosystem, including ARKit, HealthKit and Core ML.
- **Android native:** Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for robust Android experiences, with Material Design, background services and deep hardware integration.
- **Cross-platform:** React Native and Flutter for apps that share a single codebase across iOS and Android.

## Apps for every industry

- **E-commerce:** shopping apps with payments, push notifications and real-time inventory sync.
- **Health and fitness:** HealthKit and Health Connect integration and wearable sync.
- **Food and hospitality:** ordering, booking and loyalty apps with real-time updates and location services.
- **Enterprise:** field service, CRM companions and internal tools that bring the office to your pocket.

[BistroFlow](/projects/bistroflow-real-time-restaurant-pos/) is one of ours: waiters take orders on their phones with a one-handed interface, while the kitchen sees live tickets on tablets. For a restaurant chain we built a [React Native tablet app for guest ordering](/case-studies/restaurant-management-software-case-study/).

## Our mobile process

1. **Discovery and UX.** User research, wireframes and interactive prototypes validate the concept before a line of code is written.
2. **UI design.** Designs that follow the platform guidelines, Human Interface and Material Design.
3. **Development.** Iterative sprints with TestFlight and internal test builds, so you can feel the app evolving.
4. **App Store launch.** We handle submission and store optimization, then monitor and update the app after launch.
