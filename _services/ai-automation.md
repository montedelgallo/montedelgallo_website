## AI where it pays off

We implement AI technologies into your existing systems, or build new solutions from scratch. The aim is not to add technology for its own sake but to reduce friction in work that is repeated every day: the steps people copy, retype, check or chase.

- **LLM workflows** that read, classify and draft, with a person in control of the result.
- **Document processing** that turns PDFs, forms and scanned papers into structured data.
- **CRM and back-office automation** that moves information between the tools you already use.
- **Copilots** that answer questions from your own documents and data, with the source shown.
- **Agents and chatbots** connected to your business data, grounded in what your company actually knows.

## What this looks like in practice

Three of our projects show the pattern. [Chat RAG](/projects/chat-rag-ai-document-assistant/) lets people upload documents and talk to them, with every answer citing the passages it used. [Alloggiati.pro](/projects/alloggiati-pro-guest-compliance-platform/) scans a guest's ID document and extracts the required fields automatically, turning a repetitive compliance task into a fast one. [Soshiki](/projects/soshiki-multi-tenant-crm-platform/) reads incoming emails with an LLM, extracts the intent and assigns each one to the right contact or deal.

## How we work

We map the workflow first, then automate the manual steps and measure what changes. AI goes where it pays off. Our own stack for this is Ruby on Rails with PostgreSQL and the OpenAI models, and we integrate with the tools and data you already have.

If your data is not ready for AI yet, [data systems](/services/data-systems/) is where we start: pipelines, reporting and integrations that make the information reliable.
