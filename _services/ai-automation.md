## Automate the work around your documents and systems

We help businesses automate repetitive document, CRM and back-office work. If your team copies information from emails, searches through procedures or retypes document fields into another application, we can assess the workflow and build the software around it.

You can bring an existing application or a process that still runs across inboxes and spreadsheets. We start with one defined task, the people responsible for it and the result they need. That gives us a concrete way to decide whether AI belongs in the solution.

## What we can build

- **Document processing:** extract fields from PDFs, forms or scanned documents, then apply validation rules and route exceptions to a reviewer.
- **CRM workflows:** classify incoming messages, identify intent and connect information to customer or deal records, within the integrations your systems support.
- **Knowledge assistants:** search an agreed collection of documents and draft answers with references to the passages retrieved. This approach is often called retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG.
- **Back-office integrations:** connect data entry, review and updates across existing tools. Use a language model for unstructured text and explicit rules for predictable steps.

We define what the system may read, what it may change and where a person must approve an action. Drafting a reply and sending it to a customer are separate permissions.

## When AI is useful, and when it is not

AI is worth evaluating when people handle varied language or document formats and can judge a proposed result. You also need representative examples and someone who can resolve exceptions.

If a fixed rule or an API integration solves the problem, we can build that instead. Cleaning up records, standardising a form or improving search may be a better starting point than adding an assistant. We do not propose unattended decisions for a process whose errors your team cannot detect or accept.

## Start with data, permissions and a test

Together, we map inputs, existing systems, hand-offs and exceptions. Before connecting real records, we agree which data the workflow needs, who may access it and which providers receive it. We discuss retention and operational responsibilities rather than assuming privacy follows from using AI.

We then agree a bounded test with representative examples and expected results. Based on accessible data and the project scope, we agree which errors to measure, such as incorrect fields, misrouted messages, unsupported answers and cases the system should send to a person. We also agree whether and how to measure review effort, handling time and operating costs.

We compare those observations with the current process and agree acceptance criteria. If the test falls short, we narrow the scope, change the approach or keep the step manual. Any rollout includes agreed review points and a way for staff to handle failures.
