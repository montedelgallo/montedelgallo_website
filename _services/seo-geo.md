## Search engines and AI answers

SEO is optimization for traditional search engines. GEO, generative engine optimization, is the same discipline for AI engines: the assistants and answer engines that summarize the web instead of listing links. We work on both, because people now find businesses through both.

## How we build visibility

The base is structured content and entities: pages that say clearly what a business is, what it does and where, in a form that search engines and AI models can both read. On top of that sit the things that make a site easy to rank and easy to cite.

- **Content architecture** that is planned and measurable: what each page is for, and how pages relate.
- **Structured data**, such as JSON-LD, meta titles and descriptions and Open Graph, so machines read the page the way people do.
- **Performance and clean markup**, so the site is fast and crawlable.
- **Visibility across search and AI answers**, the "Rank" part of our growth system next to Build and Ship.

Our CMS engine [ActiveCanvas](/projects/activecanvas-rails-cms-engine/) includes these SEO controls, so structured data is part of how the pages are edited.

## Part of the same work

SEO and GEO sit next to [websites](/services/websites/) and the content on them. We design, code and optimize in one workflow, and then measure what changes in visibility, so the work keeps paying off after launch.
