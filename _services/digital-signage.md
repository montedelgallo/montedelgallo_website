## What it covers

Digital signage is more than a screen on a wall. A working network means monitors, the playlists that decide what each one shows, the players that run them and the infrastructure that keeps all of it running. We manage and monitor the whole chain: networks, playlists, players and infrastructure.

- **Screens and players** in the venues where your audience is.
- **Playlists** that decide what is shown, and where.
- **Infrastructure management** for the network behind the screens.

## Who it is for

We work with a single venue as well as with networks of several: retail chains and franchise locations, with more than one point of sale.

## How we work

We start from the venue and what you want the screens to do, then build the system around it. Like our other software, we measure what it delivers and keep improving it. If your network needs something specific, such as an integration with the systems you already use, [custom software](/services/custom-software/) is how we build it.

Tell us about your locations and your screens in a call, and we will define a practical way forward.
