## Data-driven decisions

Every business generates data, and few know how to use it. We bridge that gap by building pipelines, dashboards and AI models that turn your data into a strategic advantage. The goal is to make decisions clearer, not to add another tool to look at.

## What we deliver

- **Business intelligence:** custom dashboards and reports that give stakeholders real-time visibility into KPIs and trends.
- **Data engineering:** ETL pipelines, data warehouses and streaming architectures that keep your data clean and accessible.
- **AI and machine learning:** recommendation engines, NLP, computer vision and predictive models integrated into your products.
- **Analytics platforms:** end-to-end solutions, from data collection to interactive visualization and automated reporting.

Our own projects use the same building blocks. [Gestione Immobili](/projects/gestione-immobili-property-portfolio-manager/) shows occupancy, income against expenses and return per property on a financial dashboard with an interactive map, and [Soshiki](/projects/soshiki-multi-tenant-crm-platform/) brings revenue, collections, margins and expenses together for the businesses that use it.

## How we work

1. **Audit.** We assess your current data landscape, with its sources, quality and gaps, and define the opportunity.
2. **Strategy.** We design the data architecture, choose the models and set success metrics aligned with business goals.
3. **Implementation.** Pipelines, models and dashboards are built iteratively, with validation at every stage.
4. **Insights.** Ongoing monitoring, model retraining and optimization keep your data delivering value.

## Tools of the trade

- **AI and ML:** TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn, Hugging Face, the OpenAI API and LangChain.
- **Data pipelines:** Apache Airflow, Kafka, Spark, dbt and Celery.
- **Visualization:** D3.js, Plotly, Metabase, Power BI and Grafana.
- **Storage:** PostgreSQL, BigQuery, Snowflake, ClickHouse and S3.
