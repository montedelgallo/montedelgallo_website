## Sites that work for the business

We create visually striking, highly functional websites tailored to the needs of your business. They are fast, mobile-first and designed to turn qualified traffic into enquiries and sales: marketing sites, landing pages and the content that supports them.

- **Marketing sites** that explain what you do clearly and make the next step obvious.
- **Landing pages** built for a single goal and a single call to action.
- **Editable content**, with a visual editor where the site needs one (see below).

## Built to be found

A site is only useful if people reach it. Performance, content and visibility belong together, and they are the base of the work described under [SEO & GEO](/services/seo-geo/): visibility in search engines and in AI answers.

## Our own tools

For sites that need a flexible editor, we use [ActiveCanvas](/projects/activecanvas-rails-cms-engine/), our open-source CMS engine for Rails. It adds a visual drag-and-drop page builder, AI-assisted content, page versioning and SEO controls (meta titles, descriptions, Open Graph and structured data) to a Rails application. Our stack also includes WordPress with LiveCanvas, and the frontend toolkit listed under [custom software](/services/custom-software/).

## How we work

We map what the site has to achieve, build it, and then look at real outcomes to keep improving what moves the numbers.
