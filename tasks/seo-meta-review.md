# SEO titles and meta descriptions (review)

What a search result shows for each page of the site: the **title** (blue link) and the **description** (the grey text under it). The last column is the search the page is written for. They are our best guess, no Search Console data exists yet (decision D6), so please correct anything that does not match how your clients actually search.

**Limits** (enforced by the build check `script/checks/seo_meta.rb`): title up to 60 characters, description 70 to 160, both different on every page, and Italian different from English. Longer text is cut off by Google.

**How to change one.** In the page's source file, edit `meta_title` (the full title, brand included) and `description` in the front matter at the top. The home page lives in `_data/site_meta.yml`. Then run `bash script/check-build.sh`: it fails if a limit is broken. Not listed: the privacy and cookie pages, which are drafts hidden from search until the legal text arrives.
