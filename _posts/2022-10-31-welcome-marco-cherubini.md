With autumn brimming with exciting new projects, it was about time that the MdG family expanded with a new hire! Today we welcome Marco, who joins us as a junior developer. Marco brings his passion and eagerness to learn, together with a creative background in producing Hip-Hop music. We are truly excited to see what this collaboration will bring to our team and our clients!
